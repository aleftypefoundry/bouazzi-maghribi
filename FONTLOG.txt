software. This information should be distributed along with the

Basic Font Information
-----------------------

Bouazzi Maghribi is an open-source revival of a classic Arabic typeface
based on the Maghribi script, dedicated to the memory of the late
Dr. Ahmed Bouazzi. It aims to bring the elegance and detail of the
traditional Maghribi script to contemporary digital platforms, and
supports writing in both normal and Maghribi Arabic styles.

The font was designed in Glyphs App. Source files (.glyphspackage,
.designspace, .ufo) are in the /src directory; compiled binaries
(OTF, TTF, WOFF) are in /fonts.

Please refer to the Bouazzi Maghribi website/repository for the latest
version of this font: https://github.com/aleftypefoundry/bouazzi-maghribi
